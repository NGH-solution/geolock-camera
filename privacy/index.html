GeoLock Camera ("the App") is developed and published by NGH ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses or processes, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the GeoLock Camera Android app (package com.gps.geotag.live.earth.map.satellite).

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. No account required

GeoLock Camera does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any registration information to use the App. There is no login, no username/password, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number through the App itself.

2. Information the App accesses, and why

2.1 Camera and microphone

The App uses your device's camera to take photos and record video, and your microphone to capture audio when you record video. These are core features of the App and are used only while you are actively using the camera screen. This media is processed and saved directly on your device — we do not receive a copy of your photos or videos on our servers.

2.2 Location (GPS)

The App uses your device's precise and approximate location (foreground only — the App never accesses location in the background) to:

  • Burn a visible date/time/location "stamp" onto photos and videos you capture;
  • Write GPS coordinates into the photo's EXIF metadata;
  • Show your position and nearby places on an in-app map;
  • Look up a human-readable place name for your current coordinates (reverse geocoding).

Your location coordinates are processed entirely on your device and are not sent to or stored on NGH's own servers. The only place location data leaves your device is when the App asks Google's Maps Platform service to render map tiles or resolve a place name for coordinates you are viewing — this is Google acting as the map/geocoding provider, governed by Google's own privacy terms, not a transfer of your data into an NGH database.

If location services are off, or permission is denied, the App still works for taking photos/video — it simply will not add a location stamp or EXIF GPS data.

"Share without location" option. Before sharing a photo, you can turn on "Share without location," which removes the GPS EXIF metadata from the shared copy of the file. Please note this only removes the hidden EXIF GPS field — if the photo has a visible location stamp burned into the image (part of the picture itself), that visible text is not removed by this option, because it has already been rendered into the image pixels at capture time.

2.3 Photos, videos, and files

Photos and videos you capture, or import via the system Photo Picker, are saved to your device's local media storage (e.g. Pictures/GeoLock Camera, Movies/GeoLock Camera). The App does not upload, host, sync, or transmit your photos or videos to any NGH server. Deleting a file from within the App deletes it from your device's storage; we hold no separate copy.

2.4 Live street cameras

The App lets you browse a list of publicly available live street/traffic cameras. To do this, the App:

  • Requests a read-only list of camera metadata (name, location, stream link) from our content-delivery provider;
  • Checks stream availability via YouTube's public oEmbed service, and opens the stream in the YouTube app or your browser when you tap to view it.

These live streams are third-party public content that we do not host, own, or control. Viewing a stream opens YouTube, which has its own privacy policy and terms.

2.5 Notifications, network, and device signals

The App requests notification permission to show local status notifications (e.g. "export complete"), and uses standard network permissions (internet, network/Wi‑Fi state) to load map tiles, fetch the live-camera list, check for connectivity, and communicate with the advertising and analytics services described below.

4. Data retention

  • Photos/videos: retained on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We never hold a server-side copy.
  • Analytics, crash, and advertising data: retained by the respective provider (Google/Firebase, AppLovin, Meta, Adjust) according to their own retention policies, generally for product-improvement and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Since the App has no account, there is no NGH-held profile to retain or delete beyond what is described above.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: you can grant or revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, and Notification permissions at any time in your device's App settings. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature (e.g. no location stamp without the location permission).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or opt out of interest-based advertising in your device settings.
  • Consent: where the App shows a consent prompt for personalized ads, you can change your choice at any time from within the App's settings.
  • Access/deletion requests: because the App does not hold an account or server-side profile for you, there is no NGH-held personal record to request beyond the analytics/crash/advertising data described in Section 3, which is held by the respective third party. If you are in the EEA, UK, California, or another jurisdiction with statutory privacy rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.) and have a question about data a third-party SDK holds about your device, contact us at the email below and we will help direct your request or answer what we can.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored App data, including photos/videos saved under the App's own media folders (unless you have separately saved copies elsewhere).

6. Children's privacy

GeoLock Camera is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data security

Photos, videos, and location processing happen within your device's app sandbox. Network communication described in this policy uses HTTPS. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International data transfers

Where third-party services listed in Section 3 process data (e.g. Google, Meta, AppLovin, Adjust), that data may be processed on servers located outside your country, subject to those providers' own safeguards and privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy
